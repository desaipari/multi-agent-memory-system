VeriMem V2 Stage 3 Multi-Seed Replication

Seeds:
73 through 102 inclusive (30 independent controlled DEV benchmark draws).

Frozen confidence equation:
Confidence =
0.35 * SourceReliability
+ 0.30 * Corroboration
+ 0.15 * ExtractionDirectness
- 0.20 * TimeDecayPenalty

Primary VeriMem configuration:
- Learned contextual trust R(agent, fact_type)
- noisy-OR corroboration
- prior strength m = 5
- verified outcomes only update trust
- 300 verified adaptation scenarios per regime
- no CAL or TEST data

Primary metrics:
1. Conflict-only candidate-selection accuracy.
2. Newer-wrong overwrite prevention.

Secondary metrics:
- Overall candidate-selection accuracy.
- Newer-correct acceptance.
- Ambiguous low-gap rate.
- Trust convergence MAE.

Primary robustness hypothesis:
- In ALIGNED, Learned VeriMem should remain approximately comparable
  to Fixed VeriMem because the expert priors are already appropriate.
- In MIXED and SHIFTED, Learned VeriMem should outperform Fixed VeriMem
  in conflict-only candidate-selection accuracy.
- The primary effect will be evaluated using the paired seed-wise
  difference Learned - Fixed. A 95% CI excluding zero in MIXED or
  SHIFTED will be treated as evidence that the adaptive-trust benefit
  is stable across benchmark draws.

Stress hypothesis:
Learned VeriMem should prevent incorrect newer overwrites more often
than Fixed VeriMem under source-authority mismatch. This will also be
evaluated using the paired seed-wise difference and its 95% CI.

m=10 and m=20 and log-odds are sensitivity/ablation conditions and
will not replace the pre-registered primary noisy-OR, m=5 configuration
based on whichever multi-seed result is highest.
